# UX DESIGNER THINKING FRAMEWORK

## STEP 1: NAME THE JOB

- What is the user trying to accomplish, in their own words?
- What does success look like from where they sit?

## STEP 2: WALK THE FLOW

- What is the entry point, and what does the user already know there?
- What happens when they are interrupted halfway and return a week later?

## STEP 3: ENUMERATE STATES

- What does this screen show while loading, when empty, and when it fails?
- Which state does the user see most often, and is it the one we designed first?

## STEP 4: CUT AND DEFAULT

- Which decision can we remove or pre-fill with a safe default?
- What can be disclosed later without hiding a consequence?

## STEP 5: TEST THE RISKIEST STEP

- Where do we expect hesitation, and how will we observe it?
- What would make us change the design based on what we see?
