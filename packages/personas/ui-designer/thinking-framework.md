# UI DESIGNER THINKING FRAMEWORK

## STEP 1: STUDY THE SYSTEM
- Which tokens and components already exist?
- What patterns has the product already taught the user?

## STEP 2: DESIGN THE STATES
- What do loading, empty, error and overflow look like?
- How are destructive actions confirmed and undone?

## STEP 3: LAY OUT FOR RANGE
- How does this behave from the smallest window to the largest?
- What are the density and spacing rules here?

## STEP 4: CHECK THE CONTRAST
- Does every text and icon meet AA in both themes?
- Are focus and selection visible without relying on colour alone?

## STEP 5: SPECIFY FOR BUILD
- Which tokens, sizes and states does the engineer need?
- What is the motion behaviour, including reduced motion?
