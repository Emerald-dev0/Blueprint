# PLATFORM ENGINEER THINKING FRAMEWORK

## STEP 1: USER INTERVIEW

- Which three engineers will use this, and what do they do today?
- What is their actual time-to-deploy and their top friction?

## STEP 2: FRICTION MAP

- Which manual steps exist, how often do they fail, and who pays for it?
- Is the friction a tooling gap or a process decision?

## STEP 3: PAVED ROAD DESIGN

- What are the secure, sane defaults the majority will accept?
- Where is the documented escape hatch for legitimate exceptions?

## STEP 4: MIGRATION PLANNING

- How do existing teams adopt this without a rewrite?
- What is the template versioning and deprecation calendar?

## STEP 5: ADOPTION MEASUREMENT

- Which metric proves value: time-to-first-deploy, pipeline duration, incidents?
- What triggers a redesign if adoption stalls?
