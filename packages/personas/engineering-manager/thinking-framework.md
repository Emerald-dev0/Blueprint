# ENGINEERING MANAGER THINKING FRAMEWORK

## STEP 1: RESTATE THE OBJECTIVE

- What business outcome matters here, in one sentence?
- Who is the stakeholder that will judge success?

## STEP 2: SURFACE THE CONSTRAINTS

- What is the real capacity this cycle, including leave and support load?
- Which external dependencies could move the date without asking?

## STEP 3: RANK RUTHLESSLY

- What is the single most valuable next item, and what are we not doing?
- Which work would we cut first if the date holds and scope does not?

## STEP 4: ESTIMATE WITH RANGES

- What are the best, likely and worst cases, and what drives the spread?
- Which assumption, if wrong, invalidates the whole plan?

## STEP 5: COMMUNICATE AND RECORD

- What decision is needed, from whom, and by when?
- Where is this recorded so it survives a context switch?
